Privacy

What we keep, in plain words.

There are three places your information can be: this website, the AstroBots app on your computer, and the invite service. Here is each one.

This website

This site has no accounts, no trackers, no analytics and no cookies, and it stores nothing in your browser. Its fonts, scripts and pictures all come from the site itself.

There are two things to know:

  • When you open the downloads section, your browser asks GitHub (api.github.com) for the latest release. GitHub sees your network address and browser, as with any site you visit. Download buttons then take you to GitHub Releases.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and any host keeps ordinary server logs. If we move the site, this page will say where.

If you ask for an invite

The form sends your email and the note you type to the invite service. It keeps them so we can review your request and send you a code. The box that looks hidden on the form is a trap for spam robots and should stay empty.

What the invite service keeps

The invite service is the small server behind invite codes. It keeps:

Your invite
The invite code you were given.
Your device
The name your device gives itself, its platform (for example macOS or Windows) and the version of AstroBots.
Usage counts
How much each invite is used, as counts.
A shortened network address
The first three parts of an IPv4 address, kept for abuse review.
What you typed in the request form
Your email and your note.

It does not keep what a bot is working on. See the next two sections.

What stays on your computer

Your crew lives on your Mac or PC: your account, every chat, your bots' memory and files, and the bots' computers. On a Mac that is ~/Library/Application Support/AstroBots; on Windows it is %APPDATA%\AstroBots. You can open it, back it up or delete it, and updates never touch it. The invite service never receives a copy.

What goes to the model

To answer you, a bot sends the model what it is working on: your messages, and pictures of its computer's screen. With an invite, that model is hosted by the person who invited you. The invite service passes these requests along and does not keep them.

If you would rather nothing left your network, point AstroBots at a model server of your own in Settings. What a bot is working on then goes to that server instead.

Your choices

  • To remove your crew from your computer, quit AstroBots and delete its folder.
  • To have your request or invite removed from the invite service, ask the person who invited you.
