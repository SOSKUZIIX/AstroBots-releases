Install help

From download to first chat.

If your computer warns that an app is from an unknown developer, that is expected while a build is not signed yet. Once releases are signed you will not see these warnings, and the steps below simply no longer apply.

On a Mac

  1. Open the .dmg and drag AstroBots to Applications. It has to live there to update itself.
  2. Open it from Applications. If you forgot, the first launch offers to move it for you.
  3. If your Mac warns that AstroBots is from an unknown developer, open System Settings, Privacy & Security, find the message about AstroBots and choose Open Anyway. It is only needed while a build is not signed and notarized, so you may never see it.

Which Mac do I have?

Apple menu, About This Mac. A Chip that starts with Apple M means Apple silicon. A Processor that says Intel means Intel.

Updates

A signed Mac copy installs updates by itself. A copy that is not signed cannot, and AstroBots tells you so; download the new version from this page.

On Windows

  1. Run the Setup .exe. It installs for you alone, needs no administrator, and starts AstroBots when it is done.
  2. On an ARM PC it runs through Windows' own x64 emulation.
  3. If SmartScreen says Windows protected your PC, choose More info, then Run anyway. It only appears while the installer is not signed, so you may never see it.

First run

  1. Open AstroBots and type your invite code. Your invite page shows it, with a Copy button.
  2. Make your account: a name, an email and a password. They stay on your computer.
  3. Your invite connects the app to the hosted model, so there is nothing to set up. You can point it at a model server of your own in Settings instead.
  4. Give a bot a job, and watch.

Bots' computers need Docker

Each bot works on a Linux desktop of its own, and Docker runs it. Install Docker Desktop (or Colima on a Mac) and open it.

AstroBots checks for Docker, builds the computer the first time (about 600 MB and a few minutes) and tells you what is missing. Without Docker the app still opens and your bots can chat, but they have no computer to work on.

On Android

  1. Install AstroBots on your Mac or PC first. The phone app is a window into it.
  2. Download the .apk on your phone. Your browser or files app may ask you to allow installs from it, once.
  3. From then on the app offers each update itself.

On iPhone and iPad

  1. Install AstroBots on your Mac or PC first. The phone app is a window into it.
  2. Install TestFlight from the App Store, then open the AstroBots link on your device. It needs iOS or iPadOS 15 or later.
  3. TestFlight keeps the app up to date.

Add your phone

  1. On your computer, open AstroBots, then Settings, This app, Your phone and iPad, and turn on Let other devices connect.
  2. Open Settings, Account, Add a phone or tablet. It shows a code.
  3. Open the phone app and point the phone's camera at the code. No password is typed on the phone.

Your phone and computer need a way to reach each other. The same Wi-Fi works at home; away from home, a private-network app such as Tailscale does. Your bots work while the computer is awake and AstroBots is running.

Where your data is

On a Mac: ~/Library/Application Support/AstroBots. On Windows: %APPDATA%\AstroBots. Chats, memory, files and your account are in there, and updates never touch them.

To back up your crew, quit AstroBots and copy that folder.
